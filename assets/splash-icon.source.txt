splash-icon.png, android-icon-foreground.png, android-icon-monochrome.png and icon.png
are generated from assets/branding/loci-mark-pixel.png (the approved Loci path mark): nearest-neighbor 4x (5x for icon.png),
recolored to palette ink #1A1C2C (monochrome: black), centered on a 1024 canvas inside the adaptive-icon safe zone.
The background is Sweetie 16 panel #C9D8E1. Nothing is redrawn.

splash-icon.png is different: it is the FIRST FRAME of the opening animation (only the left end of the path mark, the
left checkpoint ring), so the native splash and the JS loading screen hand over without a flash. It is generated by
`node scripts/generate-splash-icon.js` from src/ui/model/mark.ts and src/ui/model/mark-trace.ts (the same grid and trace
order the app animates), nearest-neighbor 4x in the light trace colour #94B0C2 on transparency, 384 x 448, shown on the dark ink #1A1C2C splash background. Do not edit it by hand.
